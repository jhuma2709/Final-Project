Equality

All children have the right to learn. In the grips of generational poverty, children in Dhaka's slums are more often than not denied the right to a comprehensive education. Children's Hope aims to provide underprivileged children and their families equal access to a brighter future by supporting them from early schooling through to university graduation.

Empowerment

Knowledge is power. At Children's Hope we believe a comprehensive education is the key to breaking the cycle of poverty and empowering students to uplift themselves and their communities. Children's Hope works closely with students' families to build their confidence in education.

Inclusivity

Life in Dhaka's slums is a daily struggle to survive, and those with disabilities are too often unable to receive the support they need. Children's Hope strives to provide medical support to families with disabilities and facilitate community integration.

No Child Left Behind

WE WORK TO BREAK THE CYCLE OF POVERTY FOR CHILDREN IN DHAKA'S SLUMS. WE AIM TO GIVE STUDENTS THE CHANCE OF A BETTER LIFE FOR THEMSELVES, THEIR FAMILIES, AND THEIR COMMUNITY.

MICROCREDIT SCHEME

People in Dhaka's slums are refused access to loans and other basic financial services. In partnership with Prothithdhoni, we provide small, low-interest loans to start or grow businesses. Our micro-credit scheme offers a pathway to greater independence and financial stability.

SUSTAINABLE LIVELIHOODS

At Children's Hope, we prioritise family support as a critical part of ensuring children can continue their education. Through our Sustainable Livelihood Development Project, we provide grants to families in crisis, offering them stability and support.
